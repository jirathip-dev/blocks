## Summary

Decision: `timeline` draws a title column and a month of days, with bars as ink-framed cards (tone tick, progress line, always-visible grips) and weekends and holidays hatched. Move, stretch and draw follow #136's statechart: the bar lifts, snaps to days with the dates shown over it, sends one action, waits dimmed, and slides back with the app's toast when refused. The day width is a view value: fit (24–48 px) by default, and the pointer can drag only from 24 px up. Keys and VoiceOver work at every width.
Screens: a planner month inside a page with a read-only activity timeline under it; one gesture frame by frame (move, stretch, draw, refuse); a sheet of every state, zoom level, stress case and the key and VoiceOver path. Each is drawn light and dark, at 1440 and 390.
Open questions: (1) The ADR gives the block no `today` and allows no clock, so no "today" line is drawn. Should a later field add one? I recommend leaving it out for now. (2) Nested rows and segments inside a bar are left out, since the ADR's shapes have neither. Groups become separate timeline blocks under a section heading. Is that enough for the planner? (3) On phones, days stay 24 px (about 10 days in view, scrolling sideways) so bars can still be dragged. The other option is fitting the month at 8 px, which would be read-only to the pointer. I recommend 24 px.

## Rules impl follows

- Data: the ADR's `Timeline`, `TimeRow`, `Bar` and `Span` (`docs/architecture/gestures/shapes.md` §4). No field is added. `empty` defaults to "Nothing scheduled". `move`, `stretch` and `draw` each send `Span`. Fixture `fixtures/gesture/timeline.json` holds the rules' cases (keep the length on a move; at least one day on a stretch; no candidate past `from`/`to`, onto a `fixed` bar, or, for a draw, across another bar).
- Geometry: the title column is 200 px (128 px under a 600 px container). A row is 12 px plus 32 px per sub-lane, at least `--hit`. A bar is 28 px tall. Overlapping bars of a row go into sub-lanes by first fit in start order, computed from the data. The day width is `view` per block id: `fit` = the free width ÷ days, clamped to 24–48 px. − and + step through 48, 32, 24, 16, 8 and 4. Nothing is measured but the container width.
- Header levels: from 24 px up, the month band plus day number and weekday letter, with day lines. From 12 px, week numbers on Mondays and a Monday line. Under 12 px, months only (short names when a month is under 90 px). The month name sticks to the left while scrolling.
- Pointer: from 24 px up only. Grips are drawn as 2 × 12 px notches and are hit by a `--hit` square across each edge. On a short bar the body keeps its middle third. Under 24 px, a drag pans. Touch needs a 300 ms hold on a body; grips have `touch-action: none`.
- Phases (between press and settled, only `transform` and `opacity` change): pressed lifts 3 px; previewing translates to the snapped candidate, with a dashed `--edge` outline left at the start; stretch fades the bar to .35 and scales a dashed `--l` ghost from the fixed end; no-drop fades to .45; sent sits at the candidate at .55, `aria-busy`. Confirmed or refused slides to the data over `--settle`, at once with reduced motion. The candidate's tag holds the rules' words, the same text that is spoken.
- Accessibility: the block is a `group` named by its title and is one Tab stop with roving focus. A row's lane is a `list` named by the row title. A movable bar is a `button` with roledescription "bar", named "label, 7 Dec to 9 Dec, 3 days, 60 % done[, fixed]"; a read-only bar is a `listitem`. Tab goes from a bar to its start grip, then its end grip ("Start of Pour"). The keys and named actions are the ADR's (`boundaries.md`), as in states §5. A row title with `draw` is focusable and named "…, Add a bar". The zoom buttons are named "zoom out", "fit to width" and "zoom in".
- Look: tokens only, square corners. The bar fill is `--card` and the frame `--l`. Tone ticks use `--g`, `--a`, `--r`, `--c` and `--p` (`--edge` for mute). Progress is `--l`. Off days are `--line` hatching on `--bg`. There is no tinted fill and no shadow. A refusal's reason is the `toast` kind (#135).
- SwiftUI: the same layout in a `ScrollView([.horizontal, .vertical])` with pinned headers. Bars use `.gestured(…)` per `usage.md` §4, the named actions come from the rules, and VoiceOver announces the same words. The React adapter draws the same markup as the web one.
- Stress fixtures: long text (title, subtitle and label cut with "…"), empty (no rows: `empty`; a row with no bars), many rows (24 rows, overlapping sub-lanes).
- Showcase: the planner sample (`data.js`'s `planner`, with made-up names) is the gallery sample.

## Files

`timeline.css` is everything the kind adds, about 60 rules in the `blocks` layer. `timeline.js` is a port used only to draw these previews. Its `pv`, `view`, `hz`, `tall`, `meta`, `corner`, `static` and `toast` fields fake runtime state for the images and are not wire fields. It is not product code. `data.js` is the sample, and `page.css` is the host page for the previews only. Tokens come from `../neutral-default/tokens.css`, and the refusal uses `../toast/toast.css`. Images are made by `./render.sh` (Playwright's cached headless Chromium, light and dark, reduced motion).
