timeline · every state

1 · Bars

A bar is a card with an ink frame. Its tone is a 3 px tick on the left (ok, warn, bad, info, accent, mute; none draws no tick). Progress is a 2 px ink line along the bottom. A fixed bar has an edge frame, muted text and no grips. An end cut by the range is dashed and has no grip. A one-day bar with free days after it puts its label beside it, muted; otherwise the label ends in “…”. Grips are 2 × 12 px notches, always drawn, so touch users see them.

2 · Hit areas · drawn for impl only

a grip: 44 px wide, centred on the edge, the row’s full heightthe body: what is left between the grips

Each grip is a --hit square, half inside and half outside the bar. On a bar shorter than 88 px the grips overlap the body entirely, so the body is grabbed by its middle third only. Grips and handles have touch-action: none; a body needs a 300 ms hold on touch.

3 · Day width · a view value, kept per block id

fit (the default) shares the width between the range’s days, between 24 and 48 px. − and + step through 48, 32, 24, 16, 8 and 4 px; Ctrl and wheel, a pinch, or the − + 0 keys do the same. From 24 px up: day numbers and weekday letters, and the pointer can move and stretch bars. Under 24 px: week numbers on Mondays, no grips, a drag pans, and the header says “zoom in to drag”; keys and named actions still move bars. Under 12 px: months only.

4 · Stress

Long titles end in “…” on one line (the full text is the name). Bars of one row that overlap stack into sub-lanes by first fit in start order; the row grows 32 px a lane. A block with no rows draws its empty text. A row with no bars is an empty lane. Many rows scroll inside the block (70 % of the viewport) with the day header and the title column sticky.

5 · Keys and VoiceOver

The block is one Tab stop (roving focus, back on the same bar after a redraw). The same rows apply in SwiftUI, with VoiceOver’s actions rotor holding the named actions.

6 · Read-only and the static page

A timeline with no gesture field, the static site, an older client that knows the kind but not gestures: the same drawing with no grips and no handles. Bars are list items; a bar with a tap is still a button. The static page has no script, so the day width is fit and there are no zoom buttons.
