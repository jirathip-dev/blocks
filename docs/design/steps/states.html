steps · every state

1 · Step states

Done: an ink square with a tick and a 2 px ink line on. A done step with a tap underlines its name and is a button (left); without a tap it is plain (right). Current: a 2 px ink ring, bold name, "step N of M" under it. To do: grey outline and grey name.

2 · Keyboard focus, first and last

Focus on a done step: a 2 px ink ring 2 px out around its square, and its underline thickens to 2 px. The first step current: nothing done yet. No current: every step done, "All done" on phones.

3 · Stress: long text, many steps, empty

Names and subtitles keep one line and end in "…"; the full name is the step's accessible name. Five or more steps on a phone: numbers only, the current one's name on a line under them. No steps: the block's fallback.

4 · Bar values and tones

The fill is always ink. A tone colours the number and a 3 px tick at the fill's end; warn and bad also colour the note. 0 % is a grey number on an empty track. A value outside 0–100 is clamped (here 130 draws 100 %).

5 · Bars in rows, and empty

At 600 px and up a row is label | bar | number; under 600 px the label and number share a line over the bar. No bars and no value: the fallback.
