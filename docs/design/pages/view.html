pages · zoom and paging, frame by frame

Zoom and paging are view gestures (ADR #136, decision 8): they change only the block's view state, kept by block id, so a redraw keeps them. Nothing is sent to the app. Each frame is cropped to the top of the block.

1 · Fit, keyboard focus on the pages

The first draw is "fit": the widest page fills the well. Tab reaches the toolbar buttons, then the pages region (2 px ink ring). On the region: + and − zoom, 0 fits, Page Down and Page Up go a page, the arrows scroll. Disabled buttons are muted: ‹ on page 1, − and fit at fit.

2 · Next page

› or Page Down scrolls so page 2 sits under the head, over --settle (at once with reduced motion). The counter reads "page 2 of 3" and is spoken once (polite). Scrolling by hand updates the counter silently.

3 · Zoom in by button or key

Each + is ×1.25 about the middle of what is in view, which stays put. The pages scale by transform over --settle, then their width is written once; reduced motion skips the transform. Past fit the well scrolls sideways (drag, swipe or arrows); the page never leaves the block.

4 · Mid-pinch

A pinch, or Ctrl and the wheel, scales the pages by transform about the fingers as they move: following the fingers is not a transition, so reduced motion changes nothing here. The readout and the width are written once, on release.

5 · Most zoom

4× fit is the most; + and its key do nothing there and + is muted. A double tap toggles between fit and 2.5×. 0 or [fit] goes back to fit.
