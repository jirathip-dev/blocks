pages · every state

1 · Mixed sizes

One scale for the whole document: at fit the widest page fills the well, so a portrait page next to a landscape one keeps its true size. Each frame is reserved from the page's width and height before its picture loads, so nothing moves.

2 · Loading and a page that didn't load

A page still loading is a card-coloured frame at its size saying "loading page 1". One that failed says so with a 3 px brick-red edge and shows its alt text; the next draw tries again, and [pdf] is the way through meanwhile. Its accessible name still says what is on it.

3 · Many pages · the report, 60 pages

Pictures load lazily as they near the view; all 60 frames are reserved, so the scrollbar is true from the start. The counter is the only page list: no thumbnail rail. Frame cropped.

4 · Empty, and the note

No pages: the head keeps the title and [pdf] if there is a file, and the well says "No pages yet". The note is the app's line (building, failed, out of date), drawn above the pages with an ink edge; its Refresh or Sign off are the app's own buttons beside the block.

5 · Long title

A long title ends in "…" on one line; the full title is the group's and the region's name.

6 · Static page, and an older client

The static page draws the block read-only: no script, so no zoom or paging buttons; the pages at fit, a page count and the [pdf] link. A client that does not know the kind draws its fallback row, which opens the PDF.
