look

A look is a scheme pair and a style

Every page on this site wears the look picked in the bar above, and keeps it from page to page. Each look is two colour schemes, light and dark, and one style.json. The page follows your device for light or dark. Below, each part of a style, drawn in all four looks side by side.

Colour

scheme pair. The 19 colour tokens, once for light and once for dark. The page follows the device; each pair passes theme::gate.

Type

type. The font design every label is set in.

Space

space. A multiplier of every padding and gap, the card's own inside edge included, from 0.5 (Console) to 2 (Paper).

Radius

radius. A multiplier of the renderer's corner radius; 0 is square.

Motion

motion. A multiplier of every duration; 0 is none. Transform and opacity only, and none under reduced motion.

Surface

surface. How a card's surface is drawn: an edge, a hairline on the page's own colour, or a soft fill with no edge.

Fills

fills. How a tinted part is filled: the primary action, the picked chip, a tag.

Accents

accents. Whether each item in a run takes the next of five accent colours: a row's mark, a stat's number.

Marks

marks. Whether a state mark is a symbol or its word.

Sections

sections. How a section's header shows: a title, or quiet.

Labels

labels. A case and up to 4 marks of punctuation before or after a role's label: section, button, picked.

Bring your own

An app hands blocks its own pair and style the same way this site does. theme::gate checks contrast and colour distance before a scheme ships; this site's build runs it on all eight of its schemes.
