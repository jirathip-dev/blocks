## Summary

Decision: a new `sidebar` kind on the default look. On desktop it is a 64 px icon rail. Hovering it, or tabbing into it, opens it to 224 px over the page without moving the page. Pin keeps it open at 224 px and pushes the page aside, and this browser remembers the choice. It holds a brand row, links with icons, count badges (danger in red), group titles, groups that open to show their children, a footer for the account and settings, and an ink tick on the current page. On a phone it is a drawer: the header's `[≡]` slides it in, and so does a swipe from the left edge. A tap outside, a tap on a link, `[×]` or Escape closes it.
Screens: the app shell at 1440 (rail, open over the page, pinned) and at 390 (closed, mid-swipe, open), plus a sheet of every part and state. Each is drawn light and dark.
Open questions: (1) Should pin be remembered per browser (local storage, keyed by the block's id) or per account on the server? I recommend per browser: it needs no new action or round trip and works on the static site. Per account would mean an optional `pin` action the app stores. (2) A link's `icon` is a short glyph string, as `Figure.icon` already is, and two capital letters draw as an initials box (the account). Is that enough, or does the owner want a named icon set? I recommend glyphs for now.

## Rules impl follows

- Data (impl picks the final names in the crate): `type: "sidebar"`, `id`, `label` (the nav's accessible name), an optional `brand` link, `groups: [{ title?, links }]`, `footer: [link]` and `empty` (default "No pages yet"). A link is `{ label, icon?, href, current?, badge? }`. A group parent is `{ label, icon?, badge?, open?, children: [child] }`, and a child is `{ label, href, current?, badge? }`, with no icon and no children of its own. That makes one level of nesting the only one that can be built. `badge` is `{ count, tone? }`, where the tone is the shared `Tone`, and `bad` draws it in red. `nav` stays as is.
- Sizes are tokens only, on existing tokens: the rail is `4 × --s4` (64 px), the open sidebar `14 × --s4` (224 px), the phone drawer `18 × --s4` capped at the screen minus two `--hit`, and the icon cell `2 × --s3`. Rows are `--hit` tall, children `--hit − --s2`. Colours: the surface is `--card`. The edge is `--edge` (rail and pinned) or `--l` (open over the page and the drawer). Hover and current rows fill `--bg`. The current mark is an inset `--mark` tick in `--l` with `--fg` 600 text. Group titles are `--mute` at `--fs-meta`. Badges are the house `.tag`, with `.tag.bad` for danger. The scrim is `--scrim`.
- Desktop (600 px and up): the rail is fixed on the left, and the page is padded by the rail's width. Hover opens the sidebar after about 150 ms, so a pointer passing by does nothing, and it closes about 300 ms after the pointer leaves. Focus inside opens it the same way. Opening over the page never changes the page's padding. Pin sets the padding to the open width and keeps the sidebar open. Unpin goes back to the rail. Escape closes an open-over sidebar.
- Pin memory (see open question 1): stored per browser under the block's id and read before the first paint, so a pinned sidebar does not flash as a rail on reload.
- Phone (under 600 px): no rail. The screen header shows `[≡]` (aria-controls the nav, aria-expanded). Opening the drawer moves focus to its first link and keeps focus inside it. Closing it (a tap on the scrim, a tap on any link, `[×]` or Escape) returns focus to `[≡]`. A left-edge swipe runs on `gesture.rs`'s `step`: Press inside the edge zone, then Move to set the pull (0 to 1), then Release to open if the pull is past ⅓ or the flick is fast, else close. Cancel closes. A swipe left on the open drawer closes it the same way. The cross-language gesture fixtures get a case for each.
- Groups: a parent is a `button` with aria-expanded and aria-controls, never a link. The renderer opens the group that holds the current page. On the rail, children are not drawn; hover or focus opens the sidebar to reach them.
- Accessibility: the kind is a `nav` named by `label`. Group titles label their sections. Rail labels stay in the accessibility tree (only their drawing is clipped), so every link keeps its name. The current link has `aria-current="page"`. A badge reads "12 new", or "3 need attention" for danger, with the true count past 99. Icons are aria-hidden. Pin is aria-pressed, named "pin sidebar" or "unpin sidebar". Focus uses the house 2 px `--l` ring.
- Motion: width, padding and the drawer's slide move over `--enter` with `--ease`. With reduced motion they jump. No clip is needed.
- Stress fixtures: long text (one line with "…", the full text in the title and the name), empty (the `empty` text), and many rows (the body scrolls, the brand row and footer stay).
- Generic: no app names in the kind, samples or docs. The preview data here (Workspace, Inbox, Projects…) is placeholder text.

## Files

`sidebar.css` is everything the kind adds, in the `blocks` layer, on tokens only. `sidebar.js` is a port used only to draw these previews. Its `state`, `focus`, `hover` and `pull` fields fake runtime state and are not wire fields; it is not product code. `page.css`, `shell.html` (the state comes from the URL hash: `#rail`, `#open`, `#pinned`, `#closed`, `#swipe`, `#drawer`) and `states.html` are the preview pages. The tokens come from `../neutral-default/tokens.css`. The images are made by `./render.sh` (Playwright's cached headless Chromium, light and dark, reduced motion).
