## Summary

Decision: `table` becomes editable on the ADR's terms. Editable cells get a thin underline. You type, and when you leave the cell it sends one cell edit. Cells worked out by `calc`, and their totals, update as you type. The app's own cells (like a rounded final price) dim from the send until the answer. Rows move by the list's ≡ handle and send the new order. Groups are rows that carry their own subtotals and move together with their items. On a phone a row opens a sheet of fields.
Screens: the worked case (the client app's product mark-up: one cell being typed into, one sent, one row just confirmed, the phone sheet), and a sheet of every state (each phase of a cell edit, the keyboard and VoiceOver path, each phase of a reorder with groups, when rows stay put, select, phone, static page, fallback). Each light and dark, at 1440 and 390.
Open questions: (1) The ADR says a typed value the column can't take (like "8x" in Qty) sends nothing on Enter and puts the old value back, so the typing is lost. Keep that, or amend the ADR so the cell stays open until it's fixed or you press Escape? I recommend keeping it for this issue. (2) Group subtotals sit on the group's own row, not in a subtotal row under each group. OK? (3) On a phone, rows open a sheet instead of being edited in place. OK?

## Decisions

- **Within the ADR (#136).** Shapes as `shapes.md` part 4 gives them: on `Table`, `edit: Gesture`, `reorder: Gesture` and `calc` (a map from column id to `Expr`, the same as a `lines` block's); on `Column`, `edit: bool`; on `TableRow`, `fixed: bool`. A cell edit sends `CellEdit { row, column, value }` under the gesture's `name`, and a reorder sends every row id in the new order. The keys, the named actions, the motion and reduced motion are `boundaries.md`'s `edit` and `reorder` rows, with nothing added. No new op: the app answers with a `merge` of `rows`, and #80's row diff draws it.
- **One field added, the ADR's to give** ("looks and optional extras are each kind issue's design"): `TableRow.group: bool`. A group row is a heading; the item rows after it, up to the next group row, are its items. Since the order alone says which group an item is in, moving an item into another group, or a group with its items, is still one reorder result. A group row draws its name, how many items it has, and the subtotal (by the column's `total`) of its shown items for each column that has a `total`. `calc` does not run on it. The footer totals count items only, and the row count counts items. Sorting sorts items within each group; filtering keeps a group row only while one of its items is shown.
- **Three kinds of cell.** *Editable* (`edit: true`, not `fixed`, and not named in `calc`): drawn in ink with a 1px `--edge` underline. *Worked out* (named in `calc`): read-only. As you type, it is worked out by `expr` from the typed value, and so are the subtotals and totals. *The app's own* (a number or money column with neither): dimmed (muted ink, ↻) from the send until the answer, in that row, its group's subtotal and the footer total. A cell `calc` reads but no column draws (the exchange rate the app fills) is data only.
- **Cell edit phases**, the ADR's statechart: the cursor is a 2px `--l` outline. *Previewing* is the input in place. *No result* (the typed text doesn't parse for the column) gets a red left edge and its reason under the cell; Enter or Escape there sends nothing and puts the value back. *Sent*: dimmed and `aria-busy`. *Confirmed*: #80's ink edge until the next update. *Refused*: the data's value again, a red left edge until the next key or update, and the reason in the toast and spoken. While its edit is out, an edited row keeps its place in a sorted grid, then takes its sorted place.
- **Reorder phases**: *pressed* and *previewing* draw the row (or the group with its items) lifted: 2px `--l` lines around it and a 2px rise by `transform`. The other rows slide over `--enter` (with reduced motion they jump). *No drop here* (an item above the first group row) fades the row to .6 opacity. *Sent* dims it to .5 where it was dropped. *Refused* slides it back over `--settle`, with the toast. A group goes only between groups. A `fixed` row has no handle, but other rows can pass it.
- **Reorder works only in the block's own order.** A sort or a filter takes the handles away. The bar says "sorted, so rows stay put" (or "filtered") and offers [as sent]. Editing still works.
- **Lead columns**: the ≡ handle (`--hit` wide, `touch-action: none`), then #80's tick box, then the first column. All three stay put when the grid scrolls sideways. In an editable or reorderable grid, rows are `--hit` tall.
- **Keyboard and VoiceOver** (the ADR's): `role=grid` with one tab stop, which comes back to the last cell used. Arrows move the cursor. Enter or a letter starts typing. Enter sends and goes down; Tab sends and goes right; Escape cancels. A cell is named "column, row" and a worked-out cell is read as "worked out". The handle is a button named "Move <item>": Space grabs, ↑ ↓ move one place (with Shift, to the first or last), Enter drops, Escape cancels. Its named actions are Move up, Move down, Move to top and Move to bottom. Spoken positions are within the group ("2 of 3 in Valves").
- **Phone (under 600px)**: #80's phone rows with the handle first, and a › on every row. Tapping a row opens its sheet (the shell's `.b-sheet`): each column as a field row, editable ones as inputs at 16px, worked-out and app values read-only. Each field sends its own `CellEdit` when it is left (there is no draft of several), and [done] only closes the sheet. A row's own `tap`, if it has one, is an [open] button in the sheet's head.
- **Column width is not draggable** (the ADR left it to this design): `width` stays the app's. A drag would be a view gesture with nothing to send, and the client app's mark-up doesn't need one.
- **Static page**: read-only. Worked-out cells and subtotals come from the same `expr` in the site writer; no handles, no underlines.
- **Fallback**: #80's single row, with the first summed column's total worked out by `calc`.
- **SwiftUI**: #80's `Grid` (600 points wide or more), with a `TextField` in each editable cell and `@FocusState` as the cursor. Worked-out cells use the Swift `expr`. The handle is a button inside `Gestured`, with `.accessibilityAction(named:)` for the four moves. Under 600 points: #80's `List` rows, and a `.sheet` holding a `Form` of the same fields. The same phases: dimmed `.opacity` while out, the ink edge, and `.accessibilityValue("worked out")`.

## What impl builds

- In the crate: the fields above and `TableRow.group`. `kind/table.rs` gets its `Rules` (grab a cell or a row; the candidate is the parsed text, or the new order with the group rule; `says`). Then `just gen`.
- `fixtures/gesture/table.json`, run in Rust, TS and Swift, once per way in: a cell edit, an invalid one, an item moved, an item into another group, a group moved, no drop above the first group, a fixed row. `fixtures/blocks/table-grid.json` holds this mark-up trimmed, with long-text, empty and many-row stress cases.
- `web/src/render/table.ts` on `gestured`, and the rules in `grid.css` here, added to `web/src/css/kind/table.css`. A SwiftUI view and its render test. A sample on the showcase gallery.
- `grid.js` and `data.js` here are a preview-only port (they use `../table/table.js`). Images: `./render.sh` (Playwright's cached headless Chromium, light and dark, reduced motion).
