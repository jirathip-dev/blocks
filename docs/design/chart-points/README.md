## Summary

Decision: on #132's chart, a series that is not `fixed` shows a square on each point (a notch at the top of each bar). You drag it up or down, or press Space and then the arrows, or use VoiceOver's Raise and Lower. It snaps to the series' step, shows the new value and the change in a tag and in the legend, and sends one action when you let go. It waits there dimmed until the app answers, then shows the app's value, or slides back with the app's toast. A point never changes month. The axis stays fixed while you drag, so a value can't go below 0 or above the top of the axis. Line, bar and combo charts take this; stacked and donut charts don't. A chart with no `move`, with every series fixed, or whose `move` is switched off draws exactly as #132 does.
Screens: a sales plan page (a movable target line, movable crew bars, and a read-only chart beside them); one drag frame by frame; a sheet of every state, the hit areas and the keys. Each is drawn light and dark, at 1440 and 390.
Open questions: (1) Lock months one at a time? The ADR has no per-point flag. Drawn here: a fixed Actual series plus a Plan series with no values in the past months. Is that enough? (2) On a phone, a month column narrower than 24 px (a 24-month chart) doesn't grab on touch; only the keys and VoiceOver move its points, as the timeline does when zoomed out. Is that OK? (3) To raise a value past the top of the axis, you drop it at the top and drag again once the axis has grown. Is that OK, or should the axis grow during the drag?

## Rules impl follows

- **Data**: the ADR's shapes only (`docs/architecture/gestures/shapes.md` §4). `Chart` gets `move: Option<Gesture>`. `Series` gets `step` and `fixed`. The result is `PointMove {series, at, y}`, where `at` is the x value. There are no other new fields. With no `step`, a step is one hundredth of the axis top at the press (the top is always 1, 2, 2.5 or 5 × 10ⁿ, so the step is a round number too).
- **Which points move**: a point of a series that isn't `fixed`, on a `line` or `bar` chart, or on a `combo` (its lines, and its bar series when there is only one; several bar series stack in a combo). A missing (`null`) value has no point. `Built::check` refuses: `move` on `stacked` or `donut`; `move` with no series that could take it; a `step` of 0 or less; and (the core's rule) a gesture with no block `id`.
- **The rules, in Rust, TS and Swift** (`kind/core/chart.rs`, `core/kind/chart.ts`, `Kind/Chart+Rules.swift`). A grab is (series, month). A reach is a value on the y axis, from the pointer's y and the axis top frozen at the press. The candidate is the reach snapped to `step` and clamped between 0 and that top. A nudge is ±1 step, or ±10 with Shift; it gives nothing past either end. The steps are "Raise" and "Lower", left out at an end. The words are the value in the chart's `format` with as many decimals as the step needs, then "up" or "down" and the change (`฿1.3M, up ฿100K`). The tag, the legend and the spoken words all use this one string. Fixtures: `fixtures/gesture/chart.json`, with each case run once per way in.
- **Geometry**: a point's handle is a `button` with role description "point", `--hit` tall, centred on the value and as wide as its column. For a bar, the handle is centred on the bar's top and as wide as the column, and you press it by its x. The rest of the column is #132's tap column. When two movable points share a column, a press grabs the nearer one. At the press, the driver reads the column's width; under 24 px, touch doesn't grab (open question 2). Nothing else is measured.
- **Phases** (only `transform` and `opacity` change on the handle): pressed scales the square to 1.5 (a bar's notch scales sideways). While previewing, the point and its two segments follow the pointer, or a bar scales up from the baseline, and a dashed `--edge` square, segments or outline stays where it started. Sent is the candidate at .55 opacity with `aria-busy`. Confirmed or refused moves to the data over `--settle`, or at once with reduced motion. A tag (border `--l`, size `--fs-meta`) sits over the point, or under it when the point is in the top 15 % of the plot. The legend reads the grabbed month's candidate, followed by "was …" in `--mute`.
- **Accessibility**: the chart is one Tab stop with roving focus over its movable points. When `move` is on, #132's column buttons get `tabindex=-1` and `aria-hidden`, and Enter (or VoiceOver's activate) on a point sends the month's `tap` instead. The keys and named actions are the table on `states` §4, which is the ADR's `move (chart point)` row. A refusal is the `toast` kind (#135).
- **Look**: tokens only, square corners. A point is the series colour with a 2 px `--card` ring. A bar's notch is `--card`, or the series colour when the bar is too short to hold it. Focus is the ink ring `--l`. The whole CSS is `points.css`, about 20 rules added to `web/src/css/kind/chart.css`.
- **SwiftUI**: the same handles through `.gestured(…)` (`usage.md` §4), with `accessibilityAdjustableAction` raising and lowering by one step, and the same words. The React adapter draws the web markup.
- **Showcase**: `data.js`'s `plan.target` (made-up numbers) is the gallery sample.

## Files

`points.js` is a preview-only port of #132's chart with the points added, used to draw these images; it is not product code. Its `pv`, `hz`, `toast`, `static` and `tableOpen` fields fake runtime state and are not wire fields, and `move.off` stands in for an `enabled` that does not hold. `data.js` is the sample. `page.css` is the host page for the previews only. Tokens come from `../neutral-default/tokens.css`, the kind CSS from `web/dist/blocks.css`, and the refusal from `../toast/toast.css`. Images are made by `./render.sh` (Playwright's cached headless Chromium, light and dark, reduced motion).
