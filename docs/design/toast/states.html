toast · every state

1 · Tones

A 3 px tick on the left in the tone's colour and a glyph in its text colour; the surface stays the card, the frame stays ink. No tone (or a tone word this client does not know) is plain: an ink tick and no glyph.

2 · Undo, focus and dismiss

Undo is the house bracket button, lowercase, its label from the app (default "undo"). Keyboard focus: the house 2 px ink ring. × dismisses; Escape does too while focus is in the toast. Any tone may carry an undo.

3 · Stress

Long text wraps and stops at 3 lines with "…"; the full text is the accessible name. A long undo label ends in "…" on one line. Empty text draws the block's fallback ("Done" by default) so a toast is never blank.

4 · In the flow · static site, fixtures, an older client

With no server to answer, the static site writer draws the toast where it sits, as one quiet line in the flow: an edge frame, no clock, no ×, and no undo (its action has nowhere to go). This is also how blocks-shot shows the fixture.
