toast · one at a time, frame by frame

1 · Arrives · 0 s

The answer to archive carries it. It slides up from the bottom edge; the ink line under it is the time left (8 s, because it has an undo).

2 · More arrive · 3 s

Two more answers carry toasts. They wait behind it, first in first out; "+2" says so. The visible one is never replaced mid-read.

3 · Held · pointer over it, or focus inside

The clock stops and the line turns grey while the pointer is over the toast or focus is in it, so undo can always be reached in time.

4 · Undo pressed

Undo sends its action. The button is busy ("undo…") and the clock stays held until the answer comes.

5 · Undo's answer

The undo's own answer replaces the toast at once (it goes ahead of the queue). With no toast in that answer, the toast just closes.

6 · Next in the queue · warn, 6 s

When one closes (time out, × or Escape), the next waiting toast shows after 160 ms.

7 · Error · stays until dismissed

An error has no clock and no line. It stays until × or Escape, and is announced at once (alert, not status).

8 · Queue full

At most 3 wait. A fourth drops the oldest waiting ok toast (never an error), so a burst cannot pile up.
