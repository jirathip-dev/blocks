1 · The box

No icon: the look draws controls as text. Empty, the placeholder (or the label) says what it searches. With text, [clear] sits at the box's end; it is named "clear" and its hit area is the full row height. The browser's own × is turned off.

2 · Working, "3 of 12", no match

One slot before [clear] says what is happening. Live form: "searching ↻" while the app answers (the ↻ stands still with reduced motion); the box stays usable and a new key restarts the 250 ms. With filters: how many rows are left, or "no match". Muted, never red: no match is not an error. Read out politely to a screen reader.

3 · Among other fields

When the form has more than the search, it is an ordinary field row: the label on the left, as text and picker draw it. Here a picker sits beside it; both narrow the same table.

4 · The app's error, and off

The app's error is the field's red note, as on any field, with the red edge inside the box. With enabled false the box is drawn on the page colour and takes no text; [clear] is gone.

5 · Thai

The words come from the screen's locale: ล้าง, กำลังค้นหา, "3 จาก 12", ไม่พบ. Matching ignores case only; Thai has none, so it compares the text as drawn.
