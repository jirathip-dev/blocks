A confirm or alert (the alert variant)

  1. Fixed in the centre at every width, small (400 px, 320 on a phone), over a scrim. Its header has no grip and no ×, and cannot be dragged; no place is kept.
  2. Two buttons make a confirm, one makes an alert (just “ok”). A destructive confirm draws its button red and opens with focus on cancel; any other opens with focus on the main button.
  3. Escape is cancel (or ok, for an alert). Tab stays between its buttons. A screen reader hears it as an alert dialog with its title and message.
  4. Not a bottom sheet on a phone, and no swipe: a decision is never dismissed by a stray swipe.
