A tool window (opt in: tool)

  1. No scrim: the screen behind stays live, so a tool window sits beside the work. F6 moves focus between the window and the screen; Escape inside it closes it.
  2. Drag as a dialog does. Resize by the corner (both ways) or the right edge; at least 280 × 200, at most the viewport. The resize corner is a slider for the keyboard: arrows grow or shrink it 16 px, Shift 64 px. Size and place are kept together, per id, per user.
  3. Minimise (–) folds it to a pill at the bottom left, in the order they were folded. A pill is a button: click or Enter restores the window at its kept place and size, with focus on its title. A reload brings back the pills too.
  4. A dialog or a confirm has no –, no corner and no edge: only a tool window can resize or fold.
  5. Tall content goes full-screen: when the body would pass the sheet's height, the sheet takes the whole screen, still with its handle and its header's ×.
  6. On a phone a tool window is a sheet like any other: no –, no resize corner, no pills. Places and sizes kept on desktop are not used here.
