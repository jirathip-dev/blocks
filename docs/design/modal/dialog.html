A dialog (the default variant)

  1. Drag by the header, anywhere but its buttons. The grip ⋮⋮ is a hint; the whole bar is the handle. Press, move, release: the gesture machine's move, with the offset from centre as its change.
  2. Remembered per modal id, per user: on release the offset is saved in this browser under the app and the modal's id. The next “edit-item” opens where this one was left, on any screen.
  3. Clamped: on open and on every window resize the box is kept inside the viewport with a 16 px margin. Double-click the header to put it back in the centre and forget the place.
  4. Keyboard: the title is focusable; arrows move it 16 px, Shift 64 px, and a screen reader hears “moved right”. Tab stays inside, Escape closes, focus goes back to what opened it.
  5. A bottom sheet: full width, up from the bottom, never taller than the screen less two touch heights; its body scrolls.
  6. Grab handle: a swipe down on it follows the finger. Let go past a third of the sheet (or 120 px) and it closes, as cancel; short of that it springs back. Only the handle swipes, so the body scrolls freely.
  7. Screen readers: the handle is a “close” button; the header's × closes too. Focus stays inside while it is open.
