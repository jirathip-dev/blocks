## Summary

Decision: a new `modal` kind replaces `sheet` (one way to show a box over a screen) and has three variants. A dialog (the default) can be dragged by its header, and its place is kept per modal id for each user in their browser. An alert (a confirm or an alert) stays fixed in the centre. A tool window opts in to resizing and to minimising into a pill, and has no scrim. On a phone, dialogs and tool windows become bottom sheets that close with a swipe down on the grab handle and go full-screen when the content is tall. Confirms stay a small centred box.
Screens: a dialog that was dragged and remembered, a tool window that was resized with two others minimised to pills, and a fixed confirm. Each is drawn light and dark, at 1440 and 390.
Open questions: (1) Replacing `sheet` with `modal` breaks the wire. I recommend bumping the schema version and migrating `sheet`'s callers rather than keeping both kinds. (2) Positions are kept only in the browser, not synced across devices. I recommend that, since the issue's reference keeps them in local storage. (3) Tool windows are non-modal (no scrim, F6 moves between the window and the screen), so focus is trapped only in dialogs and alerts. Please confirm.

## Rules impl follows

- `modal` vs `sheet`: `modal` takes `sheet`'s fields (`id`, `title`, `cancel`, `done`, `blocks`, `locale`) and adds `variant` (`dialog`, the default, then `alert` and `tool`). An `alert` also takes `message`, `ok` and `destructive` (the same fields as the confirm part). It is a confirm when it has `cancel` and an alert when it does not. Migrate `sheet`'s callers (the crate's sample, site writer, patch, layout, and the shell's own nav and arrange sheets, which get the same look) and delete `sheet`. Impl names the final fields.
- Kept places: the key is the app plus the modal's `id`, so a modal with no id is not remembered. A dialog keeps its offset from the centre. A tool window keeps its place, its size and whether it is minimised. Everything is kept in local storage, read when the modal opens and written when a drag or resize is released. The place is clamped into the viewport with a 16 px margin when the modal opens and whenever the window is resized. Double-clicking the header puts it back in the centre and forgets the kept place. An alert keeps nothing.
- Gestures build on `crates/blocks/src/gesture.rs`. A header drag is `move`, with the offset as its change. A tool window's corner or right edge is `stretch`, with the size as its change: at least 280 × 200, at most the viewport. A swipe down on the phone handle is `remove`. Releasing past a third of the sheet's height (or 120 px, whichever is smaller) closes it as cancel; otherwise it springs back. The machine's `Send` saves the place locally, and the renderer answers it with `ok` straight away, because no action goes to the server. A release with no movement is a tap and does nothing. The machine's `Nudge` covers the keyboard: arrows on the focused title move 16 px and Shift moves 64 px, and on the resize slider they change the size. Its `says` line gives the screen-reader text ("moved right", "400 by 560").
- Focus: dialogs and alerts are `aria-modal`. Tab stays inside, Escape is cancel, and focus goes back to the opener when the modal closes. An alert is an `alertdialog` and is described by its message. A destructive confirm opens with focus on cancel; others open on the main button, or for a dialog on its first field. A tool window is a non-modal `dialog`: F6 moves between it and the screen, and Escape inside it closes it. – folds it to a pill, which is a button named "restore <title>" inside a `nav` named "minimised windows". Restoring a window puts focus on its title.
- Phone (600 px wide or less, the repo's breakpoint): dialogs and tool windows are bottom sheets. When the body would be taller than the screen less two touch heights, the sheet takes the whole screen (`full`). The handle is a "close" button with a 22 px tall strip as its touch target, and only the handle swipes, so the body scrolls freely. Alerts stay centred at 320 px. Kept desktop places and pills are not used on a phone.
- Look: tokens only. The frame is `--l`, the surface `--bg` and the scrim `--scrim`, and the rules use `--line`. The grip, the handle and the resize corner use `--mute` or `--edge`. Buttons are the house bracket buttons. The only shadow is the one used while dragging, the same one the list uses for a lifted row. In motion, a modal settles over `--enter`, or snaps into place with reduced motion.
- Stress fixtures: a long title is cut with "…" and its full text stays the accessible name. An empty body still draws the header and the buttons. Many rows make the full-screen phone sheet and the scrolling tool window.
- Generic: the samples use plain names ("Edit item", "Layers", "Delete 3 items?"), with no app names.

## Files

`modal.css` holds the rules the kind adds, in the `blocks` layer. It replaces `web/src/css/shell/sheet.css`. `page.css` and `bg.js` exist only for these previews: they draw the screen behind each modal, the purple ◆ notes and the dashed outline of the default place. `--mx`, `--my`, `--tx`, `--ty`, `--tw` and `--th` in the pages stand in for the kept places and are not wire fields. The tokens come from `../neutral-default/tokens.css`. `./render.sh` makes the images with Playwright's cached headless Chromium, light and dark, with reduced motion.
