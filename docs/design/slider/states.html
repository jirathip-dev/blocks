slider · every state

1 · At rest

The label rides above the thumb: centred over it in the middle, flush with the track's end at min and max, so it never leaves the field. The ends say min and max with the unit. No value starts at min.

2 · Moving

Pressed: a 4 px halo round the thumb and a bold label; the label changes on every step. Keyboard: a 2 px ink ring; arrows move one step, Page Up and Down a tenth, Home and End the ends. A number shows only the places it needs: 7.5 h, 12 h.

3 · Off, the app's error, Thai

Off (enabled false): the fill and the thumb go grey and the field fades as a button off does; it still says its value. The app's error: a red edge on the field and the line under it. Thai: only the label changes; the numbers and the unit are the same.
