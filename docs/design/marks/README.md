## Summary

Decision: marks are drawn over a `media` picture from the app's data, never burnt in. There are seven kinds (arrow, line, box, ellipse, text, pin, highlight), each a 3 px line with a light halo so it reads on any photo, red unless the app gives a tone. A tool in the bar above the picture draws, a tap selects, grips stretch, and [delete] removes. Each change sends one action and shows dimmed until the app answers. Every mark and grip is a focus stop with a spoken name ("Arrow 1, lower left") and the ADR's keys and named actions. The tower curve uses zoom-then-place (up to 300 %) instead of a loupe, with app-drawn fitted lines dashed.
Screens: photo mark-up in an inspection report, tracing the tower design curve, and a sheet of every state and stress case, each light and dark at 1440 and 390.
Open questions: (1) when zoomed, should arrow keys move a pin 1 % of the visible area (finer at 300 %) rather than 1 % of the picture as the ADR says? I recommend yes, as a change to the ADR's table. (2) Editing a placed text mark's words is not one of the four gestures: today it means delete and redraw. Is that enough, or should `move` carry new text? I recommend delete and redraw for now.

## Rules impl follows

- Data is the ADR's `Mark` and `MarkChange` (`docs/architecture/gestures/shapes.md` part 4); places are 0 to 1 of the picture. This design adds no field. The tool picked, the selection, the "into" group, zoom and pan are view state, kept per block id.
- Drawing: an SVG layer over the image at the drawn size. Strokes are 3 px on screen at every size and zoom (non-scaling), with a 7 px `--card` halo under them. An arrowhead is 16 px. A highlight is its tone at 32 % with no line. A pin is a 6 px dot at the exact place, with its `text` (the app's number) in a small tone tag up and to the right. A text mark is a `--card` label with a 3 px tone bar on the left, 24 characters a line, 3 lines at most. With no tone, a mark is `--r`; the tones map to `--g --a --r --c --p --l`. `fixed` marks are dashed 2 px, and dimmed while a mark of their group is sent.
- Focus and names: each mark not `fixed` is a focus stop of at least `--hit`, `role=button`, with roledescription "mark" and a name made of its kind, its number among that kind (a pin's `text`), and the ninth of the picture it starts in; a group's name comes first when it has one. A text mark is named by its words. When selected, each end of an arrow, line, box, ellipse or highlight is its own stop: "Start of …", "End of …". Named actions are G1's screen-reader-only buttons beside the mark (web) and `accessibilityAction(named:)` (SwiftUI).
- Gestures, keys and motion are exactly the ADR's table (boundaries.md), including the 300 ms hold before a touch on a mark's body drags it. The kind's own rule: a draw smaller than 2 % of the picture has no result. Lifted is a drop shadow; dragging and sent are 50 % and 45 % opacity; a refusal goes back with the reason in a toast.
- The static page and a block with no gesture fields draw the marks with no bar, no focus stops and no script. The figure is one image whose name lists the marks.
- Stress fixtures: empty (no marks, hint "pick a tool, then drag on the photo"), long text, many marks (40 pins).

## Files

`marks.js` is a port used only to draw these previews, not product code. Its `sel`, `focus`, `tool`, `phase`, `ghost`, `typing`, `zoom`, `pan`, `into` and `plain` fields fake view and gesture state and are not wire fields. `marks.css` is the look the kind adds; `page.css` is the host page. `photo.svg` and `scan.svg` are drawn stand-ins for a site photo and a maker's scanned chart. Images are made by `./render.sh` (Playwright's cached headless Chromium, light and dark, reduced motion).
