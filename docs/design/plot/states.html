plot every state the renderers draw

1 · Live in a 10 s window

Until the window is full the line fills from the left. Then the newest point stays on the right edge and the line leaves on the left. The point before the window is kept, so the line meets the left edge cut off cleanly. Tick labels move with the line and hide as they reach an edge.

2 · Static, time axis

One series, no window, no fixed range: the whole span shows. Seconds read as m:ss. One series still gets a legend, as the readout of its last value.

3 · Static, number axis with a unit

A plain number axis: the label and unit go under the right end of the x axis, and above the y ticks for y. Tick texts are bare numbers.

4 · A long session: h:mm:ss

Past an hour the ticks read h:mm:ss, in 15 minute steps here. At most 6 x ticks and 5 y ticks at any width. Under 520 px every other x label is hidden, picked by the tick's value so the labels do not jump as the line moves.

5 · Peak near the top edge

The peak is a 1 px dashed ink line across the plot, under the series. Its label is in the legend, beside a dashed key, so it never covers the line or the newest point at the right edge, and it never runs into the top edge.

6 · Peak inside the target

The dashed peak line is drawn over the band. The band is shaded moss green with a 1 px edge, and the legend names it with its range. The legend lists the series, then the target, then the peak.

7 · Long title, long series names, three series

The title and the legend wrap and are never cut. Series with no tone take ink, teal, ochre, purple, moss green in that order, as in chart.

8 · Empty

No points yet: the grid at its fixed range (or 0 to the window), and the block's empty text in the middle. No last values in the legend.

9 · An older client

A client built before plot draws the fallback row: the title and the block's summary. A client built before points asks for the whole screen each frame.
