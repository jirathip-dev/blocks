Bring your own

A scheme is the 19 colour tokens and a label. The site hands it to mount with the default pair; the look sheet lists what it is given. The gate checks contrast and colour distance before a scheme ships.

paper.json

{"label": "Paper", "color-scheme": "light",
 "tokens": {"--bg": "#f3eee4", "--card": "#fcfaf5",
  "--edge": "#d3c9b6", "--fg": "#1f1c17",
  "--l": "#24406b", "--link": "#24406b", …}}
